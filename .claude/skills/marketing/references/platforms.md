## What the apps reward
- Instagram ranks on watch time, likes per reach and sends per reach; sends weigh most with people
  who don't follow you yet (Mosseri, 22 Jan 2025) [u].
- A carousel someone skipped is often shown again from its second slide (Mosseri, 17 Oct 2024) [u].
- Carousels get about 2 times the reach, 4 times the interactions and 9 times the saves of single
  images; Reels still reach about 36% further (Metricool, 24M posts, 2026) [u].
- Slide count: 8 to 10 did best (Socialinsider 2025) [u]; 11 to 20 did best at every account size
  and 2 to 4 worst (Eden, 655,385 carousels, undated) [u].
- Words: 10 to 15 a slide, 15 to 20 at most; body text 28 to 36 px on a 1080 canvas; no large study
  (design guides 2025 to 2026) [u]. Covers: 5 to 8 words, 10 at most, a number or a named group and
  a payoff (guides 2026) [u]. Numbered lists are the strongest TikTok carousel type (2025) [u].
- Photos and carousels with music can appear in the Reels tab (Instagram @creators, Oct 2024 and
  Jun 2025) [u]. Replace Audio (21 Jul 2026) swaps a published carousel's track and keeps its
  likes [u].
- Captions and alt text from professional accounts are in Google and Bing since 10 Jul 2025 [u].
  One in four TikTok users searches within 30 seconds of opening the app (TikTok 2025) [u].
- Original work only: from 30 Apr 2026 accounts that mostly repost stop being recommended, photos
  and carousels included (PetaPixel, MediaPost) [u].
- Engagement bait, Meta's definition (Transparency Center, since Dec 2017) [u]: explicitly asking
  for votes, shares, comments, tags, likes or reactions, in five types (comment, share, tag, vote,
  react baiting). "Save this" is none of them; "send this to a Scorpio" is share baiting. TikTok
  keeps "like-for-like" and false incentives off For You [u].
- On TikTok, videos average about 5.6 times the views of photo posts (Metricool 2026, 2.3M posts)
  [u], while photo posts had 81% higher engagement in Fanpage Karma's 2025 sample [u].
- Frequency: 3 to 5 Instagram posts a week about doubles follower growth, 6 to 9 about 3.7 times
  (Buffer, Aug 2025) [u]; on TikTok more posts a week lift views per post, and the median post stays
  near 500 views (Buffer, Oct 2025) [u].
- Times to start: Instagram Wednesday and Thursday 09:00 to 12:00 (Buffer, Sep 2026) [u]; TikTok
  evenings 18:00 to 23:00 and Sunday 09:00 (Buffer 2026) [u]. After two weeks the apps' own
  follower activity decides.

## Video, for later
- Reels reach best at 30 to 60 seconds (5.6% reach rate), past two minutes about 3.5%
  (Socialinsider, 140k Reels, Jan to Jun 2026) [u]. Reels over three minutes aren't shown to
  non-followers [u].
- TikTok median views rise with length, from 1,000 at 15 to 30 s to 11,136 at 120 to 180 s, while
  engagement rate is highest at 15 to 30 s (Socialinsider, 6M videos, 2026) [u].
- The promise within 3 seconds (TikTok) [u]; about half of Reels are watched muted (Mosseri) [u],
  so captions are burned in. Creator Rewards pays only personal accounts, so not ours [u].
- Trial Reels (Reels only) go to non-followers first; the follower threshold is 200 or 1,000
  depending on the source [u].

## Sound
- Business accounts get cleared music only: TikTok's Commercial Music Library (about 1M tracks,
  organic posts and ads, TikTok only) and Instagram's Sound Collection (about 14,000 tracks, organic
  and ads, Facebook and Instagram only). Trending sounds are not licensed for a Business account's
  promotional posts (since 25 Jul 2025) [u].
- Creative Center (ads.tiktok.com/business/creativecenter), Music: filter by region, placement,
  genre, mood and duration [u]. The kit's style and search terms are in `guidelines.md` G15.
- Our own track: a TikTok video with our audio makes it our original sound; whether photo mode can
  then use it on a Business account is unclear, and on Instagram whether a carousel can use saved
  reel audio is unclear too [u]. A ten-minute test in each app settles both.
- Making it: ElevenLabs Music (trained on licensed music, commercial use on every paid plan, from
  $6 a month) [u]; Suno's paid plans licence but don't transfer ownership, with download caps since
  3 Sep 2026 [u]; Udio can't export [u]; Adobe Firefly Generate Music (20 Aug 2026) is sold as
  commercially safe [u]. A purely AI-made track has no copyright (US Copyright Office, 29 Jan 2025)
  [u], so never register it in Content ID or Meta Rights Manager. A composer on work-for-hire with
  full buyout and stems costs about $300 to $2,000 for a loop and an audio logo [u].
- Getting it into the libraries: SoundOn (TikTok's free distributor) can opt a track into the
  Commercial Music Library and Meta's libraries in 1 to 3 business days [u]; any brand could then
  use it.
- Libraries with brand licences: Epidemic Sound Business (about $30 a month yearly), Artlist Music
  Pro (about $17 a month yearly) [u]; none is exclusive.

## Posting and scheduling
- **Instagram:** the app's own scheduler (Advanced settings, Schedule): 20 slides, 3:4, music and
  alt text, weeks ahead [u]. Seven posts take about 20 minutes on Friday.
- **TikTok:** TikTok Studio on the web schedules videos only. Publer Free (three accounts, ten posts
  queued each, carousels up to 35 photos) does photo carousels, with "Add recommended sound" or a
  reminder to add our own in the app [u].
- **Posting by API ourselves.** Instagram: possible with our own Meta app in development mode, no
  App Review for our own account (Standard Access), a 60-day token refreshed on Railway, a worker
  that publishes at the time; but 4:5 only, 10 slides at most and no music on carousels [g, u].
  About two to three days to build. TikTok: an unaudited app can only post privately, the audit takes
  1 to 4 weeks, and its guidelines turn away tools that post only to your own accounts [u, g]. So
  TikTok needs a scheduler with an audited app.
- **MCP:** neither Meta nor TikTok has one (GitHub, 30 Sep 2026) [g]. Metricool's official hosted MCP
  (OAuth, a claude.ai connector, works on its Free plan with 20 posts a month) schedules Instagram and
  TikTok carousels; TikTok sound is finished in the app or TikTok picks it [g, u]. Buffer has an
  official MCP on every plan, but TikTok photo posts stop at 10 images [u]. Publer's MCP needs its
  $21 Business plan [u]. All inherit the API's limits: no music and no 3:4 on Instagram carousels.
- A cloud session can't upload to Notion until the environment's network policy allows
  api.notion.com. Until then Claude sends the slides in the chat and writes everything else on the
  rows.
