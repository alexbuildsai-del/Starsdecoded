# Guidelines

What makes a post perform and convert, from the research of 30 Sep 2026. They bend when a post
needs it, and a bend is named on the proposal page; the rules in `rules.md` never bend. The
Owner reviews them with the rules on the Social rule book page, under the same G numbers.
Sources and dates are in `platforms.md`; most were read from search summaries because the
environment's proxy blocked the pages, so they are marked [u] there. Refresh a number when a
platform or a better study changes it.

## What we make
- **G1 · The pillars.** By sign: cover, one sign a slide (Aries first), end, for any body, the Sun
  included. Computed, not guessed: cover, three to five fact slides each with its computed visual
  (the product's wheel, two standalone wheels, or the day strip with its window), end. The sky:
  the product's wheel for the moment or the day strip, made in the week it posts. The product: a
  feature, the method, how a report is made. Public figures, under rule 8. Parked: one sentence,
  two charts no score, what this child needs.
- **G2 · Carousels only for now** (Owner, 30 Sep). Video, product screens, the launch video and the
  Compatibility reel wait for the report rework, and the parent posts wait too.
- **G3 · The mix.** Seven posts a week, fourteen when a session plans two weeks, no pillar twice in a row, at least one by-sign list and one
  computed-not-guessed post. Sun-sign posts are fine, but every horoscope account makes them, so
  the Moon, the rising sign and the rest of the chart are where we stand out.

## The carousel
- **G4 · One idea per slide,** and the cover makes sense alone.
- **G5 · Readable at arm's length.** 15 words a slide at most, 10 to 15 at best. Nothing under 28 px
  on the 1080 px canvas (labels inside the product's wheel stay as the product draws them). One
  focal point, the line as the hero, light type on the dark ground, never over busy stars.
- **G6 · The cover.** 5 to 8 words, 10 at most, naming a group of people or a number and what they
  get, readable as a grid thumbnail. The words on the first slide, a planet render where it's
  relevant (rule 15).
- **G7 · Slide 2 is a second cover.** Instagram shows a skipped carousel again from slide 2. Say the
  promise again in new words and give the first payoff there.
- **G8 · Length.** Instagram 8 to 10 slides, never under 5, up to 14 for a twelve-sign list (longer
  carousels do best at every account size in the largest study). TikTok 5 to 10, or 14 for a
  twelve-sign list.
- **G9 · What gets sent.** Recognition travels: lists by sign and numbered lists perform best, and a
  sign's slide should make a reader think of one person. Put that person in the words ("For the
  Scorpio you keep texting") instead of asking anyone to send it. Mix these with posts about the
  product and the sky.
- **G10 · The last slide.** What Stars Decoded does in one sentence, one call to action (rule 21),
  the address, the mark. "Save this" is allowed; "send this to", "tag", "comment a word", "like
  if" and votes are engagement bait to both apps.

## The words around it
- **G11 · Captions.** The hook and the main search phrase in the first 125 characters (all Instagram
  shows before "more"), then one or two sentences of fact in the words people search (sign,
  placement, birth chart, personality), 150 to 300 characters in all, then the call to action,
  then three to five specific hashtags (both apps allow five). A real question is fine; asking
  for a comment is not.
- **G12 · Alt text on every slide** in both apps, from the kit's `alt.txt`. Instagram and Google read
  it, and so do people using screen readers.
- **G13 · Sizes and safe areas.** Instagram 3:4 at 1080×1440 posted from the app (4:5 at 1080×1350
  only through a scheduler's API). TikTok 9:16 at 1080×1920, text kept 250 px from the top, 430
  from the bottom, 180 from the right and 92 from the left, clear of the app's buttons (the kit
  does this).

## Sound
- **G14 · Every post gets a sound.** An Instagram carousel with music can appear in the Reels tab.
  TikTok gives a Business account the Commercial Music Library only; Instagram gives it the Sound
  Collection only. A track found on a personal account may not be there, so check it in the
  business library before it goes on a post.
- **G15 · The Stars Decoded sound.** Slow instrumental ambient electronic, an observatory at night:
  60 to 80 BPM or no beat over a soft pulse, synth pads, a quiet repeating pattern like a clock or
  a pulsar, bell tones, felt piano, a low drone. Low under the reading. No vocals, drops or trends,
  and nothing mystical: no singing bowls, harps, sitar, chimes or "healing frequencies". Until our
  own track exists, one Commercial Music Library track and one Sound Collection track in this
  style go on every post, so they become ours by repetition. No paid music tool for now (Owner,
  30 Sep). Search TikTok for "space ambient",
  "minimal synth", "arpeggio", "documentary" or "technology" (Ambient or Electronic, Calm), and
  Instagram for "ambient", "space", "minimal piano" or "cinematic ambient".

## Video, when it starts (rule 24 first)
- **G16 · Length and hook.** Reels reach furthest at 30 to 60 seconds. On TikTok views grow with
  length up to two or three minutes while engagement is best at 15 to 30. The promise is on screen
  in the first three seconds. Captions are burned in, because about half of Reels are watched
  muted. A loop ends where it starts.

## Posting
- **G17 · Cadence and times.** One a day on each app from the day the waitlist is live (Owner, 30 Sep):
  more posts lift reach per post on TikTok and follower growth on Instagram. Start with Instagram
  on Wednesday and Thursday mornings and TikTok in the evenings, in the audience's time zone.
  After two weeks each app's own follower activity decides.
- **G18 · Judge a format after 8 to 10 posts,** on saves and sends per reach, never on one post. Each
  week make two more of the top two, and drop a hook that came last twice.
