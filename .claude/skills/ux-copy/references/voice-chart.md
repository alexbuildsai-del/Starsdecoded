# Voice chart: Stars Decoded

The voice is the same on every page; the tone moves with the moment (SKILL.md). Four
concepts, each with what it sounds like and lines from our own pages. Structure after
the voice chart template in content-designer/ux-writing-skill (MIT).

## Simple words, everywhere

The Owner's standing rule (2026-10-03), for every word we write: the site, the app, the report's prose,
emails, posts, captions, mocks and specs a reader sees. Everyday words. One idea per sentence. Say it the
way you'd say it to a friend across a table. No drama, no poetry, no abstract nouns doing the work, no
metaphor the reader has to decode. If a line sounds deep, rewrite it until it sounds normal. He should
never have to ask for this again.

| Before | After |
| --- | --- |
| What you hope for is asked to go deeper or let go. | A good time to look at your plans again and keep the ones that still matter. |
| Old rules, rebuilt from the ground up | Big changes to your routines |
| Getting serious about how you come across | Taking yourself more seriously |
| A few months that ask what you're willing to carry, and what you're done carrying. | For a few months you think more about how you come across and what you take on. |
| Push and brake at once | Wanting to act, feeling held back |
| Intuition sharpens while details blur. | Ideas flow, details slip. |

## Exact

**Sounds:** precise, checkable, calm.
Precision is the brand signal: real positions, real report lines, numbers from code.
Analytical, never mystical.

Do:
- "Aquarius was rising from 01:53 to 03:03."
- "Accurate to within one arcminute, tested against NASA's JPL Horizons."
- "Every line in it shows which part of your chart it comes from."

Don't:
- "Unlock the secrets written in your stars."
- "Our powerful algorithm reveals your cosmic blueprint."

## Plain

**Sounds:** everyday, direct, spoken.
What you'd say to a friend over coffee. Astrology words only with their plain meaning
next to them.

Do:
- "Find out what your birth chart says about you"
- "If you don't know your birth time, leave it blank."
- "Your rising sign is the one that was coming up over the eastern horizon."

Don't:
- "Your birth chart, explained in plain words."
- "Leverage your natal configuration for self-actualisation."

## Warm

**Sounds:** kind, about you, never slangy.
Written to one reader about their life and the people in it. People are people.

Do:
- "Add the people you care about"
- "You still get the full report without a birth time."
- "How the two of you get along"

Don't:
- "Everyone you read joins your sky."
- "Hey bestie, the stars have tea for you!"

## Honest

**Sounds:** straight, modest about meaning, firm about limits.
Say what it is, what it won't do, and what happens when something fails.

Do:
- "No predictions"
- "The meanings come from astrology, which science doesn't back."
- "If a chapter still can't pass, we tell you what went wrong. Try again is free. If we
  still can't write your report, its credit comes back to your balance."

Don't:
- "Scientifically proven insights."
- "Oops! Something went wrong."

## Tone by moment

| Moment | Where | Tone | Example |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Curious | Home, /sample, Learn | Confident, concrete | "A sample from a real Personal report, copied word for word." |
| Doing | /sky, the birth form | Efficient | "Show my chart" |
| Unsure | Birth time, lenses | Patient | "Tell us what you have: the exact time, a part of the day, or nothing." |
| Waiting | Report being written | Calm, specific | "You can start reading the first chapters while the rest are still being written." |
| Let down | Failures | Owning it | "Our writing service didn't answer. Try again in a few minutes." |
| Deciding | Delete, pay, invite | Serious | "Delete Mira's report? Her birth details go too, unless another report uses them." |
| Finished | Saves, a report ready | Brief | "Report ready" |
