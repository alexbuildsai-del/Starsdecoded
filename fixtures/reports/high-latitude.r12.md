### Lean into

Build around the steady care and creative warmth you offer when affection feels mutual.

- **Make room for shared meals, play, and small rituals; you do your best relating when pleasure is part of daily life.** so care feels lived, not merely promised
- **Offer your patience and calm presence to people you trust; dependable closeness helps you settle and show your softer side.** so you can feel safe enough to be open

- **Notice when you keep working after the useful part is done, then feel resentful that nobody saw the extra effort.** so you can ask for recognition before exhaustion
- **Check whether you are defending an old decision because changing it feels like admitting you were wrong.** so you can respond to what is true now
