# Sign ring and degree ticks

Level: organism (a Chart layer, drawn with the `SignRing` atom). The outer ring of twelve signs; the one layer never off.

## What it replaces

NatalWheel's brass band and edges and its quadrant names; the loading story's own ring; the plates' 12-tick brass rings.

## Use it for

- Every chart; reading a sign and degree; lighting a sign (at home teal, least at ease rose, "this one" brass).

## Not for

- Houses; decoration (no glow, gradient or brass rim).

## Versions

- Slices 0.386 S to 0.478 S, `muted` at 5% and 8.5% by turns; names on a curved line at 0.4535 S in `paper-dim`; ticks every 5 degrees.
- Framing: whole sign, the rising sign's start at 9 o'clock. No birth time: Aries at 9 o'clock.

## States

Full: names and ticks. Small: slices only. A sign lit: 42% while explained, 12% after.

## Access

Part of the chart's one picture.

## Do and don't

- Don't colour signs by element, add sign glyphs or print quadrant names.
