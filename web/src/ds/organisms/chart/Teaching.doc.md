# Teaching with the chart

Level: organism (Teach). One idea on the chart the reader will see in their report, one short loop.

## What it replaces

/learn/houses' own ring (`HouseRing`); `HouseObject` icons on the chart.

## Use it for

- The House by House primer, /learn/houses, Did you know.

## Not for

- Separate teaching pictures.

## Versions

- Loop 1, your houses start at your rising sign; 2, each house is one part of life; 3, every house has a planet in charge; 4, at home or least at ease (from the engine's `COMFORT`); 5, how two planets meet (the Pair link).
- `only` names the planets the idea needs, drawn 1.25 times; `signs` lights signs teal or rose.

## States

The page shows each loop's last frame, which is also what reduced motion shows.

## Access

A caption under the chart says what it shows, one at a time, at least 1.5 s each.

## Do and don't

- Don't light two things in brass or loop faster than 1.5 s a caption.
