# Two charts: the link

Level: organism (Pair). One planet of one person at an angle to one planet of the other, drawn on both charts at once.

## What it replaces

Compatibility's two standalone wheels with a name in the middle (the `centreName` wheel keeps its name until its page moves).

## Use it for

- The compatibility walk, one link per card, beside its LinkCard.

## Not for

- Lines inside one chart; all links at once (the ledger's glyphs).

## Versions

- `focus.body` is this chart's own planet, ringed in ink, the others at 14%.
- `guest`: the other's planet at its true degree on the outer lane, half size, 55%, a dashed ink ring.
- Ticks to the inner circle (the guest's dashed), the faint shape (triangle, square, hexagon), the side: teal straight (easy), rose zigzag (rubs), a brass dot when the same spot; the angle in the middle, mono grey.
- 132 px phone, 186 px desktop; names and ticks from 150 px; no horizon, no R, no lines.

## States

One link at a time.

## Access

Each chart is its own image with the person's name.

## Do and don't

- Don't give each person a colour, draw a line from one chart to the other, or show two links at once.
