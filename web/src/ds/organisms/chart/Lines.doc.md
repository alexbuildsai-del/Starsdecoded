# Lines between planets

Level: organism (a Chart layer). How the planets face each other: the engine's list, never a second one.

## What it replaces

Nothing in NatalWheel (the colours, strength and dashes are kept); the loading story's dashed paper lines.

## Use it for

- Full, Build, Live sky; Focus with the subject's own lines.

## Not for

- Links between two people (TwoCharts), Pair, Small, Chiron and the nodes.

## Versions

- From each planet's degree on the inner circle (0.146 S), straight.
- Joined `brass`, easy (60, 120) `line-easy`, tense (90, 180) `line-tense`.
- Strength s = 1 − orb ÷ limit (the report's orbs, else the engine's); width (0.6 + 1.2 s) × S/600; opacity 0.18 + 0.42 s.
- Dashed when parting, solid when closing in; strongest first.

## States

- Focus: own lines stay, the rest at 10%.
- No birth time: the Moon's lines left out.

## Access

Lines are part of the picture; nothing to focus.

## Do and don't

- Don't add arrows, labels, teal or rose.
