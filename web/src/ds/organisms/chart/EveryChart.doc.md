# Every chart

Level: organism (map). Every place the product draws a chart today and the state that replaces it; the planner searched again before building.

## What it replaces

- House by House desktop, /sample (`NatalWheel` via `HouseDeck`): Full, then Focus.
- House by House phone bar (`HouseDeck`, 92 px): Small.
- Opening plate (`ReportHero`): Sun, Moon and rising. Dashboard sky card and sample head (`TriadPlate`, `SampleHead`): the same.
- Compatibility's two charts (`CompatibilityReportPage`): Pair.
- Report and pair loading (`BuildStory`, `PairStory`): Build.
- Did you know (`DidYouKnow`): Small.
- Home hero, sky screen, /sky (`HorizonWheel`): Live sky. Home claims (`Claims`): Focus.
- Two plates (`TwoPlates`): Sun, Moon and rising, twice, one horizon.
- /learn/houses (`HouseRing`): Teach; `HouseObject` icons leave the chart and stay as decoration.
- /sky Read the wheel and placements: Focus.

## Use it for

- Each group 3 card switching its screen to `<Chart>`.

## Not for

- Not charts: the closing, the two charts ledger's glyphs, the age ring, the people orbit, the Ask mark.
