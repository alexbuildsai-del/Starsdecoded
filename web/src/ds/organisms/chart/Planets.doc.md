# Planets

Level: organism (a Chart layer, drawn with `PlanetBodyMark`). Each planet is its render; Chiron and the nodes are grey drawn points.

## What it replaces

NatalWheel's brass points, leader lines and tick dots; the loading story's dots; each plate's own Sun and Moon sizes.

## Use it for

- Where each planet stood; the subject of Focus; the guest of Pair.

## Not for

- Dots or letters; renders over 90 px.

## Versions

- A square 0.05 S wide on its lane (0.324, 0.266, 0.208 S), centred on its degree; backing disc `ground` at 92%.
- Lanes from `assignLanes`: crowding moves a planet in, never off its degree.
- R: mono, `back`, off in Pair and Small. Teach draws planets 1.25 times.

## States

- Focus: the subject ringed in brass, others at 14%. Pair: own planet ringed in ink, others at 14%.
- Pointed at: brass ring and chip, `<Planet> · <deg>°<min>′ <Sign> · <nth> (<word>) · going backwards`.

## Access

A planet stop keeps today's name: "<Planet> <degree> degrees <Sign>, house <n>".

## Do and don't

- Don't draw points in brass or add a leader line.
