# Horizon and rising marker

Level: organism (a Chart layer). The line across the chart, east on the left, and the marker on the rising degree. Both need a birth time.

## What it replaces

NatalWheel's ASC, MC, DSC and IC lines and labels; the plates' angle glyph markers and dashed horizons; HorizonWheel's MC line.

## Use it for

- Every chart with a birth time, in every state but Pair.

## Not for

- The MC: no MC line, label or marker anywhere. A chart without a birth time.

## Versions

- From the inner circle (0.146 S) to 0.398 S each side, through the rising degree. East 1.5 × S/600 at 45%, west 1 × S/600 at 25%; never through the middle.
- Marker on the outer edge: a ring max(3 px, 0.0142 S) filled with the ground, edge paper, a dot at 35%, a tail 0.02 S outward.

## States

Lit: the marker brass only while house 1 is lit (the rising sign is the subject).

## Access

The chart's name says ", horizon not drawn" when there is none.

## Do and don't

- Don't write "ASC", dash the horizon or shade below it.
