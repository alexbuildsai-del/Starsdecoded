# Houses

Level: organism (a Chart layer). Whole sign: each house is one whole sign and house 1 is the rising sign, drawn inside the ticks.

## What it replaces

NatalWheel's own highlight colour (lit is brass); HouseRing's labels and marker.

## Use it for

- "Your Venus (10th, career)"; lighting the house being read; teaching how houses count.

## Not for

- A chart without a birth time: none drawn.
- Pictures of rooms: `HouseObject` is decoration beside a word, not on a chart.

## Versions

- Slices from 0.38 S to 0.146 S, paper at 1.2% and 2.6% by turns.
- Word on a curved line at 0.411 S: `N · WORD`, capitals, 0.019 S, paper at 55%.

## States

Lit: brass 22% (35% in Small, so it reads at 92 px); its word at 100%, the others at 30%.

## Access

A house stop is "House <n>, <Sign>".

## Do and don't

- Do say a house as number and word; light one at a time.
- Don't draw cusps at other degrees, or houses without a birth time.
