# Chart drawings

Level: organism (review). Which state covers each of the 23 charts drawn today, the gaps, and the interactive states.

## What it replaces

The Review 09/10 chart parts, settled into nine states.

## Use it for

- Checking a screen's chart against the state it should take.

## Not for

- The three gaps: the Timeline dial, the share image (words only, on purpose), print (Full on white needs print colours).

## Versions

Full, Focus on a house, Focus on a planet, side by side.

## States

- Point at a planet (hover, keyboard focus, tap): brass ring and chip. Nothing dims.
- Move between houses (Tab): brass outline on the focused house, 1.5 px, no fill.
- Pick a house (click, Enter, Space, tap, a swipe of the deck): Focus, the house brass 22%, its word white, the others 30%, planets outside it 14%.

## Access

The focus outline is brass, drawn in the house's own shape (MB-196 provisional: the site's focus colour stays everywhere else).

## Do and don't

- Do keep every body at its true degree (R-3.1).
- Don't invent a state for a gap.
