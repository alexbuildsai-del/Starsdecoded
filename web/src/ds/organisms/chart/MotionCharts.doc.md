# Chart motion

Level: organism (motion on the Chart). The gathers, first light, the rewind, the plates, the house ring, the orbit and the dial keep their timelines; their curves and durations read the motion tokens.

## What it replaces

Nothing of the motion itself: each stays in its file (M4 to M52 in the Motion pages).

## Use it for

- Build's order: sign slices from Aries, names and ticks, planets running to their degrees, lines strongest first, the birth time turning the sky, horizon and marker, houses in opposite pairs.

## Not for

- Moving a body off its degree at rest.

## Versions

Build's last frame is Full.

## States

Reduced motion shows the last frame, still.

## Access

Motion is never the only signal.

## Do and don't

- Do end every motion on a state from the table.
