# RetrogradeBadge

A 22 px square R in `back` (18 px with `size="small"`), beside the planet or block that goes backwards. Hidden from assistive tech unless `label` is set, for a badge with no word beside it. On a chart the R is drawn by the Chart part in the same `back`.

Fates: P9 one badge; P10 the standard; P12 replaces the "Rx" text; P13 the dial's own dashed ring and R stay.
