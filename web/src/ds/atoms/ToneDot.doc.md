# ToneDot

A 9 px dot beside its word, never alone. Tones: heavy (rose), mixed (paper-dim), light (teal), credit (violet, one per credit). `hue` overrides the fill for a one-off colour (P5's Waiting for Sam, #9FA8DA). Hidden from assistive tech: the word carries the meaning.

Fates: P1 the standard; P2 kept as credit dots.
