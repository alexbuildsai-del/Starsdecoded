# Logo

Mark, Wordmark and the app icon in one place.

## Props
- `kind`: `"mark"`, `"wordmark"` or `"icon"` (the Mark on a ground square, 14 px corners). `className`.

## Do / Don't
- Space around it at least the size of the brass point. Ring in indigo, point in brass.
- No gradient, no glow, no stretching or rotating.
