# ChoiceTile

## Level
Molecule.

## Replaces
B11: the two big options on the dashboard (Make a report, first steps). It was a hand-made button in Space Grotesk.

## Use it for
- Choosing one of two actions of the same weight, side by side at one width and height.

## Not for
- Three or more options. Use SegmentedControl or a list.
- A single action. Use Button.

## Versions
- Main: `indigo` fill with white text; the group's one main action differs by colour only.
- Plain: `control-edge` outline on the page.

The title is Inter 500 at 15 px, like every button (Settled 9). The line under it is 12 px.

## States
Rest, hover, press (scales to .98, off under reduced motion), focus, disabled.

## Access
- Each tile is at least 56 px tall.
- The edge is `control-edge`, 3:1 or more; text is 4.5:1 or more in every state.
- A real `button`: Enter and Space work.

## Do and don't
- Do give both tiles the same size.
- Don't make both main.
