# Strip

## Level
Molecule.

## Replaces
C15: the raised band that quotes the reader's report word for word (Ask answers). The "For you" line of the cycle card uses it too.

## Use it for
- A quote or a short "For you" line inside a card.

## Not for
- Anything the reader should press. A Strip never acts.
- Long text. Past three lines, make it a Card.

## Versions
One. `raised` fill, no edge, corner `control`.

## States
Static.

## Access
- `paper` on `raised` is above 4.5:1; a caption inside it uses `paper-dim`, 12 px or more.

## Do and don't
- Do give the source in a caption inside the Strip.
- Don't give it an edge; the lighter fill is enough.
