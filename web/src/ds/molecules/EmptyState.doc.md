# EmptyState

## Level
Molecule.

## Replaces
New part. Each empty list wrote its own words and box.

## Use it for
- An empty circle, no pairs yet, no transits this week.

## Not for
- An error. Use Alert or InlineError.
- A list that is still loading. Use Skeleton.

## Versions
One. A dashed `line` edge at the card corner, a label, a title, one line, and the one action.

## States
- With an action.
- Without an action (nothing the reader can add, such as a quiet week).

## Access
- The title is a heading; the action is a Button with its own 44 px tap area.
- The dashed edge is decoration only.

## Do and don't
- Do name what will appear and give the one action that adds the first.
- Don't offer two actions of the same weight.
