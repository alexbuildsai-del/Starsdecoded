# Voice study: how @the_innercosmos explains astrology, and what we take from it

Source: auto-captions of 114 of her TikTok videos (Dec 2025 to Oct 2026, about 42,000 words).
Checked against `api/src/prompts/system.ts` (DOCTRINE, STYLE_CONTRACT) and `api/src/prompts/vocabulary.ts`.
This file is not in the repo on purpose. Her words are hers. Quotes here are short fragments used as evidence only.
Every crisp line and scene below is ours. Caption errors are corrected silently ("Mercury and Gemini" = Mercury in Gemini).

## Headline findings

1. **Her real-life scenes are rarer than they feel.** About 45 to 55 concrete scenes across 114 videos. Most sit in the
   "random astrology observations" lists (about 45 of the 114 videos) and in the sign-run lists (Mercury, Mars, Moon by sign).
   The other 70% of her talk is abstract: "self expression, your inner child, your ego". The scenes stick because they are rare and short.
2. **Her strongest unit is one line long.** Placement as a person, one behaviour, one exaggeration. "Gemini Mercuries could talk to a wall."
   No setup, no theory. The theory, when it comes, comes after.
3. **She varies the format, not the theory.** Her stock theory lines repeat a lot (Saturn "won't let you cut corners", Jupiter "does not
   discriminate", 12th house "where the ego goes to die"). What keeps her fresh is that each video uses a different slicing (planet by sign,
   planet by house, sign by house, template, analogy) and each item in a list lands in a different life setting.
4. **Her rulers are mostly modern.** Scorpio by Pluto, Aquarius by Uranus (she says "I think it's both"), Pisces and the 12th by Neptune,
   and a "natural ruler" for every house (Leo for the 5th, Capricorn for the 10th). Our doctrine uses traditional rulers and whole-sign houses only.
   Her house-ruler method (find the sign on the house, then find its ruler) is the same as ours, and that part we can use as is.
5. **The scene plus the crisp line is exactly what our report is missing for Saturn and Jupiter.** Our vocabulary says what Saturn is.
   It never shows Saturn at a desk at 11 p.m. Section 3 gives three of each.

How this maps to the report: the style contract keeps planet, sign and house names out of prose. So in a report the crisp line becomes
a label or claim, and only the scene and the "one thing to do" go into the prose, in second person. In posts and "Explained" cards,
the crisp line can name the planet.

The order inside one good item of hers is almost always: **placement, behaviour, exaggeration, then (sometimes) the why.**
Her house-by-house videos invert it: **theory first, then the list.** Those are her weakest videos. The Owner's favourite lines all come from the first shape.

## 2. Scene types for real-life examples

A scene is a short moment the reader can check against their own week. Each type below shows a different side of a person.
The point is variety: a writer picks the scene type that fits the placement, and never uses the same type twice in one report.
"She used it" means the type appears in her corpus. The rest are open ground for us.

Two notes for the writer:
- **Match the scene to the house before the planet.** The house names the setting (the 6th is Monday morning, the 11th is the friend group).
  The planet names the action. The sign names the style. This alone stops "party" from showing up for every chart.
- **Her best scenes contain a number or an object.** "Five to 10 business days", "300 ideas", "a spreadsheet", "silky pajamas".
  A small concrete detail is what makes a scene feel seen, not the drama around it.

## 3. Crisp line per planet, hers and ours

Format: how she frames it (paraphrase plus a fragment), then our crisp line, then one fresh scene of ours, then the doctrine check.
In posts, use the crisp line as written. In reports, the crisp line stays out of prose. Only the scene and the action go in, in "you".

### Sun
- **Hers:** core identity and ego, the father; "everything else revolves around the sun".
- **Ours:** The Sun is what you want your life to be about.
- **Scene:** Someone at a wedding asks what you do. The Sun is the answer you wish you could give, not your job title.
- **Doctrine:** One of the three anchors. Leads in a day chart. In a night chart it still gives direction, but the Moon leads.

### Moon
- **Hers:** your emotional inner world, who you are "when nobody's looking"; also the mother.
- **Ours:** The Moon is what you reach for when the day goes wrong.
- **Scene:** A rough day at work. You get home and either cook something slow, ring your sister, or go straight to bed. That first move is the Moon.
- **Doctrine:** The body, habits and needs. Leads in a night chart. Domicile Cancer, exalted Taurus, detriment Capricorn, fall Scorpio.

### Mercury
- **Hers:** how you think, speak and learn; her best list, Mercury by sign as party guests and note-takers.
- **Ours:** Mercury is how you think, and how it comes out when you speak.
- **Scene:** The group chat asks "who's in for Friday?" One person answers in two seconds. One drafts the reply three times. One asks who's driving.
- **Doctrine:** Neither day nor night. Takes on the nature of planets it sits with. Domicile Gemini and Virgo, exalted Virgo, detriment Sagittarius and Pisces, fall Pisces.

### Venus
- **Hers:** love language by house, your colour palette, also money and charm.
- **Ours:** Venus is what you enjoy and how you make people feel welcome.
- **Scene:** A friend shows you their new flat. You notice the light, the cushions and the smell of the candle. Then you say exactly the nice thing they hoped someone would say.
- **Doctrine:** The helpful planet of the night side, kinder in a night chart. Domicile Taurus and Libra, exalted Pisces, detriment Scorpio and Aries, fall Virgo.
  "Love languages" are her modern add-on, not doctrine.

### Mars
- **Hers:** action, anger, drive; Mars by sign as fight styles; "where you're gonna do your best work".
- **Ours:** Mars is how you go after what you want, and what you do when someone blocks you.
- **Scene:** Someone takes the parking space you were waiting for. You either honk, get out and say something, or drive off and replay it all evening.
- **Doctrine:** Helpful by night, harsher and costlier by day. Domicile Aries and Scorpio, exalted Capricorn, detriment Libra and Taurus, fall Cancer (she gets the fall right).

### Jupiter (extra depth)
- **Hers:** "the Santa Claus planet", growth, luck, protection. Her sharpest point, used at least four times: Jupiter grows the bad along with the good.
  She also says Jupiter and Saturn together means blessings come only after Saturn's work.

**Our three crisp lines**
1. Jupiter is where you say yes before you check the calendar.
2. Jupiter is the part of life where help tends to show up when you ask.
3. Jupiter is where you are generous, sometimes with time you don't have.

**Our three scenes**
1. A friend mentions they're moving on Saturday. Before they finish the sentence, you've offered your car, your morning and your cousin's van.
2. You apply for the job you're not quite ready for. You get the interview. Someone there remembers you from a course you took for fun.
3. It's the first week of January. You've signed up for a gym, a language app and an evening class. By March one of them has changed your life, and you've paid for all three.

**How to use it in a report (doctrine)**
- Sect first. In a day chart Jupiter does the most good: lean on scene 2 (help arrives, doors open). In a night chart it is a little less steady: lean on scene 1 or 3 (saying yes too fast, too much at once).
- Dignity next. Domicile Sagittarius and Pisces: the generosity is real and backed up. Exalted Cancer: big-hearted and sometimes promises more than it can give.
  Detriment Gemini and Virgo: it works hard and the results are uneven, so the yes needs a follow-up. Fall Capricorn: the person doubts that good things will come and builds the structure first (she says this too).
- House names the setting. Jupiter by house, or the house Jupiter rules, is where the scene happens. Jupiter's joy is the 11th: friends and allies.
- What to say plainly, which our report never says: "This is where things tend to go your way. It's also where you take on too much."

### Saturn (extra depth)
- **Hers:** "the Lord of Karma", tough love, delays, no shortcuts; whatever you build there lasts; rewards come late.
  Her lines on it are her most repeated (she uses the corner-cutting line at least four times).

**Our three crisp lines**
1. Saturn is the part of life where nothing comes free, and what you build stays.
2. Saturn is where you're scared, and where you get good slowly.
3. Saturn is the job you give yourself that nobody asked you to do.

**Our three scenes**
1. Everyone else is winging the presentation. You made notes, a backup file and a printed copy. Nobody asked you to, and you'd do it again.
2. At 24 you're saving while your friends travel. At 38 you own the flat, and they're the ones asking how you did it.
3. Someone says "just text him back". You've written the reply four times. It's still in drafts at midnight.

**How to use it in a report (doctrine)**
- Sect first. In a day chart Saturn does steady, useful work: lean on scene 1 or 2 (care, patience, results that last). In a night chart it is harsher and brings more fear:
  lean on scene 3 (holding back, doubt), and pair it with what helps.
- Dignity next. Domicile Capricorn and Aquarius: the discipline is natural and trusted. Exalted Libra: fair, careful, sometimes too careful.
  Detriment Cancer and Leo: hard work for uneven results, often around feelings or being seen. Fall Aries: the person doubts their own patience.
- House names the setting. Saturn's joy is the 12th: it is steadier working alone and out of sight than people expect.
- What to say plainly, which our report never says: "This is the part of life you'll have to earn. It's also the part that will hold up best."
- Her "karma for anyone who messes with you" video is not doctrine and we never use it.

### Uranus
- **Hers:** rebellion, sudden change, a thought dropped "like a grenade".
- **Ours:** Uranus marks the part of life that changes without asking you first.
- **Scene:** You had a five-year plan at work. One phone call in March, and by summer you're doing something else, and it suits you better.
- **Doctrine:** Colour only, by house. Rules nothing, no dignity. Never a chart ruler (she makes it Aquarius rising's ruler; we don't).

### Neptune
- **Hers:** dreams, fog, illusion; where you are most confused and also where a dream can come true.
- **Ours:** Neptune marks where you see what you hope is there.
- **Scene:** You fall for a flat from the photos. The view is real. So is the damp patch you didn't see until move-in day.
- **Doctrine:** Colour only, by house. Never a ruler of Pisces or the 12th for us.

### Pluto
- **Hers:** death and rebirth, power struggles, obsession, the area you'll "destroy over and over".
- **Ours:** Pluto marks the part of life you can't do halfway.
- **Scene:** You meant to try one pottery class. Six months later there's a kiln in the garage and you've stopped answering texts on Sundays.
- **Doctrine:** Colour only, by house. Never a ruler of Scorpio or the 8th. No trauma claims (she says Pluto in the 4th means "the most traumatic childhood"; we never say that).

### Chiron
- **Hers:** "the wounded healer", a chip on your shoulder that never fully goes.
- **Ours:** Chiron marks a sore spot you end up helping other people with.
- **Scene:** You still freeze before you speak in meetings. You're also the one who writes the new hire a short script and says they'll be fine.
- **Doctrine:** A modern point. Colour only, by house. No dignity, rules nothing.

### The nodes
- **Hers:** the soul's growth path; the South Node as a past-life gift, mastered "like advanced calculus".
- **Ours:** The South Node is what you fall back on. The North Node is what you're not good at yet, and need.
- **Scene:** You always plan the trip yourself, down to the train times. The trip you still talk about is the one a friend planned and you just turned up.
- **Doctrine:** Read as one axis, by house and sign. Not planets. No past lives.

## 5. Her "observations": verdicts against our doctrine

Verdicts: **True** (fits our doctrine or astronomy as stated). **Tradition-only** (a real idea in some school, fine on a "Did you know" card worded as "often read as", never as fact).
**False** (wrong by our doctrine or by astronomy, or her own invention dressed as rule: never use).
Every draft is worded as tradition, never as fact.

| # | Her observation | Verdict | Did-you-know draft (ours) |
|---|----------------|---------|---------------------------|
| 1 | The Sun is the father, the Moon the mother. | Tradition-only. Old texts use the Sun and Saturn for the father, the Moon and Venus for the mother, by day or night. | Did you know? The Sun is often read as the father and the Moon as the mother. Older books swap in Saturn and Venus, depending on the time of birth. |
| 2 | Night chart: if the Sun is below the horizon you act more like your Moon. | True in idea (sect). Her house test is rough. | Did you know? Charts are often read as "day" or "night" ones. In a night chart, the Moon is read as the lead, not the Sun. |
| 3 | Mars in Cancer is in its fall, Mars in Libra in detriment. | True. | Did you know? Every planet has a sign where it's at home and a sign where it struggles. Mars is often read as weakest in Cancer. |
| 4 | Mercury does not do well in Pisces. | True (detriment and fall). | Did you know? Mercury is often read as at its best in Virgo and most lost in Pisces. Same planet, opposite signs. |
| 5 | Cancer is where the Moon feels at home. | True (domicile). | Did you know? Each sign is often read as "belonging" to one planet. Cancer belongs to the Moon. |
| 6 | A planet in detriment or fall works in ways nobody expects, not just weaker. | True in spirit (detriment works hard for uneven results). | Did you know? A planet in a hard sign is often read as working harder, not as broken. |

| 8 | Saturn is a "malefic". Saturn rewards late. | Tradition-only. | Did you know? Saturn is often read as the planet of slow results. Many old texts link it to later life. |

## 6. Anti-repetition

### How she avoids sounding the same across 114 videos
1. **She rotates the slicing.** Planet by sign (Mars, Mercury, Moon editions), planet by house (Saturn, Pluto, Venus), sign by house (Gemini, Leo, Scorpio "where you have it"),
   pairs (Saturn with Jupiter), axes (3rd and 9th), and topics (dressing, money, ick, love). Same theory, new slice each time.
2. **She rotates the format.** Plain list, fill-in template ("it's all fun and games until"), analogy explainer (car, cake), news (transits), case study (celebrity charts).
   About every fifth video changes shape.
3. **Inside a list, each item lands somewhere new.** In her Mercury list: honesty, stubbornness, a party, opening up, a crowd, receipts, arguing, testing questions,
   big opinions, small talk, devil's advocate, imagination. Twelve signs, twelve different settings. This is her best habit.
4. **She mixes the item shape.** Advice ("bring a..."), warning ("do not ask a..."), confession ("I say this as..."), ranking ("my favourite..."), myth-bust ("people say...").
5. **Where she fails:** her theory lines repeat word for word. Jupiter "does not discriminate" (4 times), Saturn won't let you "cut corners" (4), "where the ego goes to die" (3),
   "LA LA land" (3), "the Lord of Karma" (3), and the same closing line in 94 videos. A reader of our report would notice that kind of repeat in one sitting.

### Five rules for our writer
1. **One scene type per report, once.** Keep a running list of the scene types used (section 2). A report never uses the same type twice.
   The house picks the setting first, so two placements in different houses land in different settings by default.
2. **The scene must need this chart.** Every scene carries one detail that only fits this sign in this house in this condition
   (for example, Saturn by night in the 3rd: the reply that stays in drafts). If the same scene would fit any chart, rewrite it.
3. **No crisp line or scene is reused from a fixed bank.** The lines in this study, the vocabulary and the brief are models, never text.
   This already sits in style contract rules 3 and 9. The lab should check for any 6-word run shared with this study or with another section of the same report.
4. **Rotate the shape of each section.** Not every section opens with the behaviour. Vary across: the scene first, the habit first, the cost first, the "one thing to do" first.
   No two sections in a row use the same opening shape.
5. **One concrete object or number per scene, never the same one twice.** A spreadsheet, a drafts folder, a parking space, "four times", "7:40 a.m.". Ban a short list of
   overused props across all reports (group project, party, spreadsheet, receipts) unless the placement truly calls for it, and then only once.
