# Teaching with the chart

We explain an idea on the chart the reader will see in their report. One idea, one short loop. A new idea gets a new loop here.

## Rules for every loop
- One caption at a time under the chart, simple words, at least 1.5 s each; hold the last frame 2 s or more.
- A real chart from the engine (sample: Thibault; in the report, the reader's own).
- Teach state: only the layers the idea needs; planets at 1.25 times.
- Runs only on screen; reduced motion shows the last frame and its caption, still.
- Brass is what the caption is about; teal and rose only for at home / least at ease and for links.

## The loops
1. **Your houses start at your rising sign** (8 s): the sky turns 150° into place under the flat horizon; houses count 1 to 12; house 1 brass.
2. **Each house is one part of life** (18 s): one house lit (brass 22%), its word large in the middle, what it covers in the caption.
3. **Every house has a planet in charge** (10.2 s): the 7th (Capricorn) → Saturn in the 8th; the 4th (Libra) → Venus in the 10th; the 2nd (Leo) → Sun in the 11th. A dashed brass curve from the house to the planet.
4. **At home, or least at ease** (12 s): see `Colour/`.
5. **How two planets meet** (12 s): two planets at their real degrees (Beatrice's and Athena's links) on a dial; own planet solid ring, guest dashed; 0° same spot (brass), 60° hexagon side and 120° triangle side (teal), 90° square side (rose zigzag).

## Do / Don't
- Do caption what the chart shows at that moment.
- Don't draw separate teaching pictures, light two things in brass, or loop faster than 1.5 s a caption.
