# Observations inbox

Every idea from the Owner's sources, sorted three ways: waiting for a second source, left out and why, and in reports (the
last section, a readable copy of `api/src/prompts/observations.ts`). An idea waits for a second independent source before it enters the table
(ADR-403, review-08-10 §9). `/observe` reads the Notion page "Observations inbox", adds what it finds here, and moves an idea
into the table once a second source holds and the idea checks out against the doctrine. Each line keeps the source's good
wording, lightly adapted so it is not a word-for-word copy (the Owner, 2026-10-09).

A source counts when it is another creator, an article or a book, each account or author once, or our doctrine when the
planet's meaning and the house's or sign's in `vocabulary.ts` carry the reason on their own. A search result is a lead, never a
source, and content farms never count. Hype, forecasts, promised outcomes and guesses about a reader's past never enter (R-5.2).
Keys follow `ObservationKey`; "none" means the table has no key for it yet.
